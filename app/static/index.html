    About TouchGrass Buddy

    An open-source companion designed to encourage physical outdoor habits and reduce screen fatigue.

    Local AI Architecture

    Text generation and photo verification run on your local computer using Ollama.

    • Mission Generator: Uses llama3.2:3b to produce structured physical actions.
    • Vision Judge: Uses moondream to verify mini-challenge photographs.

    If Ollama is offline or uninstalled, built-in fallback routines provide immediate mission suggestions.

    External Weather & Mapping Services

    To keep you informed of real-world outdoor conditions without requiring paid subscriptions:

    • Weather: Forecasts and precipitation probabilities are fetched via Open-Meteo.
    • Parks & Trails: Real outdoor amenities and green spaces are queried through OpenStreetMap Overpass.
    • Map Tiles: Displayed using Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles.
    Privacy & Data Guarantees

    Your preferences, habits, and excursion photos never leave your device.

    • Photos taken for verification are processed by the local vision model in memory and compressed in browser storage.
    • Missions, streaks, XP, and badges are stored strictly in local device storage.
