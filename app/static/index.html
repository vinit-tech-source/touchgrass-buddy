    About

    TouchGrass Buddy is an open-source tool designed to help you disconnect from screens and explore the outdoors.

    • Local AI: Mission text generation and photo verification are processed entirely locally on your device using Ollama. No third-party AI APIs are used.
    • Privacy: Your preferences and photos remain on your machine.
    • External Services: We use Open-Meteo for free weather/geocoding and OpenStreetMap (Overpass) for local park discovery. Coordinates are sent to these services.

    Architecture: Python, FastAPI, HTML/JS, Leaflet.
